OUR STORY

Founded in 2016, our organization expanded into Puerto Princesa three years ago through the creation of the PPC MAD BARRACUDAS FAMILY SWIM TEAM.

Today, September 2026 marks the beginning of a new chapter — with new management, a new coaching direction, and new swimmers moving forward together.
