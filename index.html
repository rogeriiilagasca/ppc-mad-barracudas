WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN A SWIM TEAM.

Mad Barracudas Family Swim Team began in 2016. Three years ago, the organization expanded into Puerto Princesa through the creation of PPC MAD BARRACUDAS FAMILY SWIM TEAM.

2016

The Beginning

The Mad Barracudas Family Swim Team was founded, creating the foundation for a family-centered swimming community.

THREE YEARS AGO

A New Branch

The Barracudas family expanded into Puerto Princesa with the creation of PPC MAD BARRACUDAS FAMILY SWIM TEAM.

2026

A New Chapter

PPC MAD BARRACUDAS FAMILY SWIM TEAM enters a new chapter with new management, a new coaching direction, and a renewed team environment.
